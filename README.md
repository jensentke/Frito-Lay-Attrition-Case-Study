# Frito-Lay-Attrition-Case-Study
Case Study for DS6306

Case study considering attrition against 870 employees using ~30 variables by employee  
Company Attrition at 16.9%, industry benchmarks below 10%-15% depending on business cycle would be considered healthy  
Uses EDA, KNN, Naive Bayes to predict attrition  
Key Attributes: Job Role, Involvement, and Level aka progression in company  
Secondary Factors: Age, Compensation, Years at Company and in role and with manager  
Position Factors: Overtime and Stock Option availability  

KNN Model with only key factors considered: 84% accurate, 99% sensitivity, 9% specificity  
    With probability threshold updated to 20%: 80% accurate, 86% Sensitivity, 46% Specificity  

Naive Bayes with only key factors: 76.6% accurate, 80% Sensitivity, 51% specificity  
    With all data, 40% probability and monte carlo: 78.5% accurate, 81% sensitivity, 64% specificity  

Number of factors and analysis included in powerpoint, however costs of attrition are tied to actual attrition and incentive program  
No other factors or "exit interview" information provided post-attrition, so limited understanding of how to prevent attrition and lower cost  
Incentive program impact also unknown and cost of it dwarfed by attrition costs.  

Many remaining questions on unknowns, however numerous recommendations provided to focus on cost reduction and improved hiring outcomes
